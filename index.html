THE HOLLOW HOUSE

a survival horror

October 3rd. Your car died on Route 9, and the only light for miles came from an old farmhouse. You remember knocking. You remember his smile.

You wake in a guest bedroom that locks from the outside. The man who lives here — the one the papers call the Butcher of Marrow County — is somewhere below, and he is always, always hunting.

Find the three emblems that seal his front door. Stay quiet. Hide when you must. And whatever you hear in the dark… keep moving.

YOU ESCAPED

Cold rain has never felt so good. Behind you, a scream of rage shakes the windows of the Hollow House.

To whoever wakes up in this room —

He sealed the front door with a lock of his own making. Three emblems: a WOLF, an OWL, a SERPENT. Slot all three into the front door in the foyer and it will open.

I found the wolf in the kitchen. I saw the owl on his desk in the study. The serpent is in the bathroom — he keeps it locked; look for a rusty key on the fireplace mantel.

He hears running. He sees light. The wardrobes are deep enough to hide in.

Don't end up like me.
