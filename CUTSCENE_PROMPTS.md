# The Hollow House — Cutscene Prompt Pack 🎬

Copy-paste prompts for generating video cutscenes (Higgsfield or any AI video
tool) for every story moment in the game. Everything here is our own original
story and characters.

## How to use these

1. Paste the **STYLE BLOCK** at the start of every prompt so all clips match.
2. When a character appears, paste their **CHARACTER BLOCK** word-for-word —
   same description every time = same-looking character across clips.
3. Ask for **16:9, 8–12 seconds** unless a prompt says otherwise.

### STYLE BLOCK (paste into every prompt)
> Cinematic live-action horror film style, 16:9, moody and grounded, heavy
> film grain, shallow depth of field, muted desaturated colors with warm
> practical lights, slow deliberate camera moves, no text, no captions.

### CHARACTER BLOCKS

**THE BUTCHER (Silas Crane):**
> a huge hunched man in near-black work clothes and a blood-stained butcher's
> apron, a stitched burlap hood over his head with a crooked too-wide grin
> sewn into it, faint red glowing eyes, a heavy cleaver in one fist

**THE WIDOW (Crane's wife):**
> a tall gaunt elderly woman in a floor-length dark-green mourning dress,
> waist-length loose gray hair, a cracked pale face with deep hollow eye
> sockets and tiny amber pupils, a small brass locket at her throat, a
> burning iron lantern always in her left fist

**THE PLAYER'S HANDS (first-person shots):**
> first-person view, a teenager's hands with cut knuckles, purple-blue
> bruises, thin red scratches and one grubby blood-spotted bandage, teal
> hoodie sleeves

## CHAPTER ONE

**1. Opening — the car dies on Route 9** (12s)
> [STYLE] Night, pouring rain on a lonely country road. An old car rolls to
> a dead stop, headlights flickering out. Through the windshield, far across
> a dark field, a single farmhouse window glows warm. Slow zoom toward that
> distant light. Thunder.

**2. The knock** (8s)
> [STYLE] [HANDS] First-person: a bruised hand rises and knocks three times
> on a weathered farmhouse door in the rain. A long pause. The porch light
> above buzzes on. The door creaks open onto darkness. Cut to black on the
> fourth knock.

**3. Waking in the guest bedroom** (10s)
> [STYLE] [HANDS] First-person: eyes open on a water-stained ceiling. The
> view sits up slowly in a dim guest bedroom — peeling striped wallpaper,
> a boarded window flashing with lightning, a note lying on the floorboards
> by the door. Distant heavy footsteps below. Held breath.

**4. Him, at the end of the hallway** (8s)
> [STYLE] A long dark farmhouse hallway lit by one failing bulb. The light
> dies. A lightning flash through a window reveals [THE BUTCHER] standing
> motionless at the far end, head tilted. The light stutters back on — the
> hallway is empty. The bulb swings gently.

**5. Caught — the Butcher's kill** (8s)
> [STYLE] [THE BUTCHER] looms over the fallen camera in a dark hallway,
> raising the cleaver high. Three fast slashes whip across frame with red
> spray on the lens. The view tips sideways to the floorboards. His boots
> step closer. Black.

**6. The woman in the corner** (8s)
> [STYLE] A dim living room lit by fireplace embers. A woman in a gray
> nightgown stands perfectly still facing the corner, long black hair down
> her back, whispering. The camera creeps closer — her head snaps around
> impossibly fast. Hard cut to black with a screech.

**7. Escape — the front door opens** (12s)
> [STYLE] [HANDS] First-person: bruised hands slam three metal animal
> emblems — wolf, owl, serpent — into slots on a huge double door. Locks
> thud open one by one. The doors groan apart onto blue pre-dawn rain and
> dark pine trees. The view runs out into the cold air. Behind, a furious
> roar shakes the house.

## CHAPTER TWO

**8. Dawn on the estate** (12s)
> [STYLE] Wide aerial-drift shot at misty dawn: a dark boarded farmhouse at
> the forest's edge, a winding dirt path lit by small warm lantern-posts,
> pines in low fog, a river with a wooden footbridge, and far off in a
> clearing — a cozy cream-and-teal campervan with glowing windows and
> string lights. Birdsong.

**9. The phone call** (10s)
> [STYLE] [HANDS] First-person inside a warm cluttered campervan — string
> lights, posters, a rumpled pink-blanketed bed, a wood stove flickering.
> An old black rotary phone rings on a small desk. A bruised hand lifts the
> receiver slowly to the ear. Static crackles. A woman's voice begins,
> too quiet to make out.

**10. Crossing the river** (8s)
> [STYLE] [HANDS] First-person: bruised hands shove a heavy fallen pine off
> a wooden footbridge; it rolls and splashes into the fast gray river below.
> The view crosses the creaking planks through drifting mist toward a tall
> crooked two-story house on the far bank, one upstairs window faintly lit.

**11. Her lantern, through the walls** (10s)
> [STYLE] Inside a rotting hoarder's house — junk piles, moss, black mold,
> roaches scattering from the light. Down a long hall, warm lantern-glow
> slides along the wall from around a corner, growing brighter.
> [THE WIDOW] steps into the doorway, lantern raised, head tilting slowly.
> Hold on her amber eyes.

**12. Caught — the Widow's kill** (8s)
> [STYLE] [THE WIDOW] rushes the camera with impossible smoothness, lantern
> flaring. Thin parallel claw-scratches rake across the lens. Her cracked
> face fills the frame. The world irises to black around the lantern flame
> until only the flame is left. It goes out.

**13. Stealing the serum** (8s)
> [STYLE] [HANDS] First-person close-up: a bruised, bandaged hand lifts a
> small glowing red vial from an apothecary shelf of dusty jars, then a
> glowing green vial from a dead planter. Somewhere in the house, lantern
> light stops moving. Held breath.

**14. The angry call** (8s)
> [STYLE] The campervan phone rings violently, almost rattling off the
> desk. A bruised hand grabs it. On the line: a low furious male voice
> growling through clenched teeth, then a scuffle, a door slam, and a calm
> tired woman's voice taking over. The player's grip tightens on the
> receiver.

**15. The blue lamp** (10s)
> [STYLE] Dusk-blue river mist. Far down the bank, a cold blue lamp
> flickers alight on an old leaning boathouse at the end of a wooden dock.
> Slow push along the water toward it, past reeds and dark pines. The
> current hurries the other way.

**16. The handoff — ending** (12s)
> [STYLE] On the boathouse dock, a hooded woman with empty hands waits at
> the rail. Bruised hands pass her the red and green vials. She mixes them
> in one glass — the liquid turns the color of dawn. Behind the pines, a
> distant door slams. She looks up past the camera. Cut to black.

**17. To be continued** (8s)
> [STYLE] A small boat pulls away from the dock into river mist at sunrise,
> the crooked house and the dark farmhouse both shrinking behind the pines.
> On the far bank, barely visible between trees, a huge hooded figure
> stands watching the boat go. Slow fade to black.

## CHAPTER FOUR

The game looks for two more clips: `return` (played as Chapter Four opens)
and `burning` (played as the house catches). Drop them in
`assets/cutscenes/` and add them to `CUT_VIDS` in `js/game.js` — or via
`window.HH_VIDEO_URLS = { return: [...], burning: [...] }`. If the files
aren't there, the game skips straight to the dialogue.

**THE DROWNED MAN (Crane, given back by the sea):**
> a huge hunched man in soaked near-black work clothes and a waterlogged
> butcher's apron, a rotting burlap hood plastered wet to his head with a
> crooked too-wide grin sewn into it, faint pale ice-blue glowing eyes,
> dark kelp tangled over his shoulders, constantly dripping, a rusted
> cleaver in one fist

**18. `return` — back up the river at dusk** (12s)
> [STYLE] A small fishing boat noses up a darkening river at dusk, pines
> going black against a bruise-colored sky. On the deck, a hooded woman
> watches the water behind the boat, not the way ahead. Far off on a rise,
> a dark farmhouse waits with its front door standing wide open. No lights
> anywhere in a crooked house on the far bank.
>
**19. The lantern goes out** (8s)
> [STYLE] Inside a rotting hoarder's house, an iron lantern hangs from a
> hook, burning alone in the dark. No one is holding it. The flame shrinks,
> gutters, and goes out by itself. In the after-dark, the sound of slow
> water dripping somewhere below the floor.

**20. Him, coming home along the riverbed** (10s)
> [STYLE] Underwater, moonlight rippling down through black river water.
> [THE DROWNED MAN] walks slowly along the riverbed against the current,
> trailing kelp, boots stirring silt. He does not swim. He walks. Above
> him, the shadow of a small boat passes. His pale eyes tilt up to follow
> it.

**21. Caught — the Drowned Man's kill** (8s)
> [STYLE] A pitch-dark farmhouse hallway, rain hammering the boarded
> windows. A flashlight beam whips around to find [THE DROWNED MAN]
> already filling the corridor, dripping. The rusted cleaver rises. Three
> fast slashes whip across frame. The flashlight rolls on the floorboards,
> its beam full of falling water drops. Black.

**22. `burning` — the match** (12s)
> [STYLE] [HANDS] First-person: a bruised hand strikes a match on the
> porch rail of the dark farmhouse at night. The tiny flame drops in slow
> motion onto oil-soaked boards. Fire races away along the porch and up
> the boarded windows with a soft WHUMP. In an upstairs window, a huge
> hooded silhouette stands with both hands flat against the glass, not
> moving, as the flames climb around it.

**23. Into the storm** (12s)
> [STYLE] Wide shot at first light: the farmhouse fully ablaze against a
> paling sky, sparks rising into the last stars, three small figures
> walking away down a dirt road without looking back. The fire's roar
> fades under morning birdsong. Then hard cut to night again — a car's
> headlights on a flooded moor road, and a black castle on the ridge.

## CHAPTER FIVE

The game looks for one clip here: `castle` (played as Chapter Five opens).
Drop it in `assets/cutscenes/` and add it to `CUT_VIDS` in `js/game.js`,
or via `window.HH_VIDEO_URLS`. If it's missing, the game goes straight to
the dialogue.

**THE RED COUNTESS:**
> a tall elegant ancient noblewoman in a floor-length blood-crimson gown
> with a high black gothic collar, long straight black hair, a pale
> beautiful gaunt face with black-lidded eyes and glowing red irises, a
> thin blood-red circlet on her brow, long clawed pale fingers, blood at
> the corner of her mouth

**24. `castle` — trapped at Ravenmoor** (12s)
> [STYLE] Night, torrential rain on a bleak moor. Three soaked travellers
> run up to an enormous black gothic castle whose great iron doors already
> stand open, warm candlelight within. As the last of them steps inside,
> the doors swing shut on their own and a heavy bolt drops. Slow push down
> a vast candlelit stone hall hung with blood-red tapestries and grim
> portraits, blood streaked down the stonework.

**25. The Countess descends** (10s)
> [STYLE] A grand gothic staircase lit by iron chandeliers. [THE RED
> COUNTESS] descends slowly, one pale clawed hand trailing the banister,
> crimson gown pooling on each step, red eyes fixed on the camera, a small
> cold smile. Candles gutter as she passes. She stops, tilts her head, and
> whispers a welcome.

**26. Caught — the Countess feeds** (8s)
> [STYLE] [THE RED COUNTESS] moves impossibly fast across a stone chamber,
> gown flaring, and seizes the camera. Her face fills the frame, mouth
> opening far too wide over pale fangs. The frame floods deep red; a single
> drip runs down the lens; then black.

**27. Dawn at last — the true ending** (12s)
> [STYLE] The great iron doors burst open onto a pale gold sunrise over the
> moor. Three exhausted figures stumble out into real daylight as, behind
> them in the dark doorway, [THE RED COUNTESS] shrieks and recoils from the
> light, dissolving into dust and shadow. The doors boom shut on an empty
> ruin. The three walk down the hill toward a distant road. Slow fade to
> warm white.
