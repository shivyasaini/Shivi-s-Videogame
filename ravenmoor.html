THE COUNTESS

RAVENMOOR

a gothic vampire horror

Your car dies on a flooded moor road at midnight. The only light for miles is a black castle on the ridge — its great doors already open, as if it were expecting you.

The moment you and your two friends step inside, the doors bolt shut behind you. This is the house of the Red Countess, and she has been lonely, and hungry, for a very long time.

She is a vampire, and the old rules still bind her: the sunrise will end her. Find the iron key in her dungeon, ring the dawn bell to throw open every shutter — and let the morning in. Hide in the coffins when she drifts near, and whatever you do, do not let her mouth reach your throat.
